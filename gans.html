GANs Video

A video demonstration of the GAN image-generation project.

GANs works by simoultaneously training two neural networks, a Generator and a Discriminator.
The Generator attempts to learn facial patterns and is rewarded for tricking the Discriminator. Meanwhile, the Discriminator is being trained and rewarded for accurately distinguishing a real photo from image generated.
Theoretically, in the long-term a GANs Generator might become overfit, where the Generator has learned to effectively memorize the dataset and outputs images which fool the Discriminator due to being nearly identical to a dataset image.
In a more ideal training time, however, the Generator creates faces which draw on the pattern of what a human face tends to look like, interpolating between all the faces within the dataset. gansvideo.mp4.
