Digital Antfarm

Inspired by Dwarf Fortress and Langton's Ant, the "Digital Antfarm" started with just playing around with moving ants, represented by an 'A' on a grid. Over time it grew into a simulation of a fantasy world of terrains, objects, and animals.

← Back to Projects

General gameplay

Animals move each with their own priority queues. They move according to a A* pathing algorithm to a targeted tile, object, or animal. The green path shows each animal's intended path and right clicking on a tile gives the stats and relevant information.

Themes and Terrains

Video shows the menu which allows you to up the tickspeed of the gameplay, change the visual themes, or generate a new gameplay terrain. There is also an option to change the gamplay from "Creative" or Sandbox mode to Adventure mode, where the player can no longer place and move units and items, but instead has a beacon which they can play to guide Human to objectives. In this Adventure mode, there are "pack" items which, when picked up by a Human, give the play a small number of cards which can be placed as items or equipment.

Lightning Scepter

The following videos are demonstrations of a few of the "scepters" which are generally the strongest magical items in the game.

Ice Scepter

Demonstration of the ice scepter effect. The ice scepter generates icicle terrains which thaw after an allotted number of ticks. Icicle terrain inflicts damage and a freeze effect which can prevent a unit's actions.

Fire Scepter

Demonstration of the fire scepter effect. Fire in this game is modeled as a terrain which can randomly go out or spread to adjacent tiles. Fire is much more likely to spread to nearby trees or wood items and can also cook meats. Humans will often prioritize putting out fires. Notably, Dragons breathe a purple "magic fire" which doesn't spread. This was a change I had to make because otherwise dragons could easily light the entire world on fire.

Creative Bar

This is the Creative Bar of all the terrains, objects, and animals within the game. Each of these can be dragged from this bar onto a tile to instantiate it, or an entity can be dragged onto the X at the end to delete it. Terrains: Water, Mountain, DungeonWall, Wall, Door, Chest, Fire, MagicFire, IcePlain, Lava, Magma The terrains include things like the Wall, Door, and Chest because the default behavior of Humans is to gather objects, cut down trees and gather wood, and then construct houses using that wood. Animals: Ant, Tree, Ent, Tiger, Rhino, Human, Werewolf, Dragon, Skeleton, Necromancer Animals such as Ent and Werewolf will disguise as a tree/ werewolf until certain conditions are met. The necromancer is another unqique entity which summons skeletons bound to it. Objects: Sword, axe, bow, book, robe, meat, pelt, wood, pack, beacon, fireScepter, iceScepter, lightningScepter, gravityScepter, necromancerScepter, chestplate Each of these items is equiped, some weapons can be dual-wielded, such as the axe, to stack their damage increases. Some weapons deal bonus damage to certain enemies, such as the axe dealing bonus damage to trees and ents. Objects like the book and robe enable humans to convert into subclasses, specifically the wizard and monk subclass. These subclasses change their priority queues and give them access to unique abilities, such as a fireball, a teleport, and passive healing.

Focus

Digital Antfarm experiments with visual systems, simulation-like behavior, text-based aesthetics, and interactive tool effects.
