Button Battle Game

A little godot project inspired by some videos I saw.
The game consists of a variety of circles, or buttons, which bounce around in an arena with unique effects to damage each other.

← Back to Projects

Gameplay Screenshot

This screenshot shows the Apiarist fighting the Conductor.

The Apiarist works by creating flowers across the arena. Whenever it bounces into these flowers it generates two bees which fly towards the opponent, stinging and poisoning them.

The Conductor works by creating a train track behind itself after hitting a wall. It will continue to create these tracks until it hits the opposite wall, at which point a train runs down the track, potentially hitting the opponent.
